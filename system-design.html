A repeatable interview method

Design outside-in, then pressure-test.

01 · RequirementsClarify users, core flows, consistency, latency, availability, durability, privacy, and exclusions.
02 · ScaleEstimate requests, read/write ratio, objects, bytes, growth, fan-out, hot keys, and geography.
03 · ContractsDefine APIs/events, idempotency, pagination, errors, versioning, and authorization boundaries.
04 · DataChoose entities, access patterns, indexes, partition key, replication, retention, and consistency.
05 · ArchitectureDraw request, data, and control flows. Keep ownership and trust boundaries explicit.
06 · Deep diveExplore the hardest bottleneck: coordination, hot partition, fan-out, ordering, or failure recovery.
07 · FailureApply timeouts, retries, backpressure, isolation, failover, reconciliation, RPO, and RTO.
08 · OperabilityDefine SLOs, signals, rollout, migration, capacity, cost, audit, and disaster drills.
